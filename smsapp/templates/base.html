ABOUT US

Seth Anandram Jaipuria School, Barabanki is committed to ‘Empower, Enthuse and Excel’ in providing quality education and ensuring every child is taught the necessary values to guide him/her through the journey of life. We at Seth Anandram Jaipuria School endow education for LIFE!
