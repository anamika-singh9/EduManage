SCHOOL RULES & REGULATIONS

To maintain a safe, disciplined and productive learning environment, students are expected to observe the following:

  • Attend school regularly and reach classes on time; a minimum attendance percentage is required each term.
  • Wear the complete school uniform, neat and well-maintained, on all working days and for physical education classes.
  • Bring the school diary and required textbooks/notebooks as per the day's timetable.
  • Treat teachers, staff and fellow students with courtesy and respect at all times.
  • Mobile phones and other electronic gadgets are not permitted inside the classroom unless required for a class activity.
  • Any leave of absence must be communicated in advance through the school diary or by informing the class teacher.
  • Damage to school property, ragging, or bullying of any kind is strictly not tolerated.

For the complete, detailed set of rules applicable to a specific class or session, please refer to the student handbook issued at the time of admission.
