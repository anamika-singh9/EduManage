Fee Structure

FEE STRUCTURE

School fees are charged on a termly basis and cover tuition, learning material and the facilities used through the academic year. Fees can be paid online, by cheque, or in person at the school's accounts office, within the due dates announced at the start of each term.

The exact fee amount varies by class and is revised at the start of every academic session. For the current session's detailed fee schedule, please contact the accounts / admission office.

Once a student is enrolled, their fee payment and due amount are tracked directly in the Student Portal of this School Management System, so parents and students always have an up-to-date view of what has been paid and what remains due.
