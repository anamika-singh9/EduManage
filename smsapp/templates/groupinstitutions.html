GROUP & INSTITUTIONS

Seth Anandram Jaipuria School, Ved Campus – Barabanki is part of the Jaipuria group of educational institutions, which runs schools and higher-education institutes with a shared commitment to quality education and value-based learning.

Each campus follows the group's ideals while serving the needs of its own community — at Barabanki, this means bringing a modern, English-medium education within reach of students in the region.
