Academics

OUR APPROACH TO LEARNING

The school follows an activity-based, interdisciplinary approach to teaching, moving beyond lectures towards discussions, projects, quizzes and hands-on classroom activities. Every classroom is IT-enabled, and technology is woven into everyday lessons to encourage active learning and conceptual understanding rather than rote memorisation.
Life Skills & Value Education
Alongside the academic curriculum, students take part in regular Life Skills and Value Education sessions guided by trained counsellors. These sessions build confidence, empathy and decision-making ability, helping every child grow into a well-rounded individual and not just an exam performer.

Our Teachers
Teachers are recruited through a rigorous selection process and are encouraged to keep learning themselves — reflecting on their own teaching style so they can better understand how each student learns. In the classroom, teachers act as facilitators and mentors rather than the sole source of knowledge, working alongside students to build curiosity and independent thinking.
