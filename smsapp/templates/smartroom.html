SMART CLASSROOMS

Every classroom is equipped with a Digital Smart Board, turning everyday lessons into interactive, multimedia experiences. Teachers use videos, simulations and interactive exercises alongside traditional teaching to make abstract concepts easier to visualise and understand.

Along with smart boards, the school's technology-enabled campus includes computer labs, a language lab and access to online learning resources — helping students become comfortable with the digital tools they'll rely on well beyond the classroom.
