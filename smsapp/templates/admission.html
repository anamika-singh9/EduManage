ADMISSION PROCEDURE

Admission at Seth Anandram Jaipuria School, Ved Campus – Barabanki is a simple, transparent process open to all eligible children regardless of caste, creed or background. It follows these broad steps:
  1. Registration: Fill out and submit the admission enquiry form, available at the school office or through the Contact Us page.
  2. Document Verification: Submit the required documents (birth certificate, previous school records, address proof, passport-size photographs) for verification.
  3. Interaction: An age-appropriate interaction / assessment is scheduled with the child (and parents, where applicable).
  4. Confirmation: On successful selection, the admission is confirmed after the requisite fee is paid within the given timeline.

For class-wise seat availability and the current academic session's admission calendar, please contact the admission office directly.
